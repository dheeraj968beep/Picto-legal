# Privacy Policy — Slay & Scroll

**Effective date: 14 November 2026**

Slay & Scroll ("the App") is a short-video app: you can watch short videos,
post your own, like and comment, follow creators, and discover new videos.
The App is operated by an individual developer based in India.

Contact for privacy questions and requests:

**Email: slayscroll73@gmail.com**

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Account information (you give this to us when you sign up)

- Email address
- Username
- Password — stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text
- Display name
- Date of birth — used only to check that you are 18 or older (see
  section 6). Your date of birth is never shown publicly and is never
  returned by the App's programming interface.
- Profile photo (optional)
- Bio (optional, up to 150 characters)
- Interests you pick during onboarding (optional)

### 1.2 Content you create

- Videos you upload, and their captions. Videos are stored in cloud
  storage and are public — anyone using the App can watch them.
- Likes, comments and replies, follows, and shares.
- View counts on your videos.

### 1.3 Reports and blocks (safety)

- Reports you file about a video or a comment (the reason you pick and
  any note you add) and users you block. We use these to review content
  and keep the App safe. A video or comment reported by three different
  users is hidden automatically until it is reviewed.

### 1.4 Advertising — Google AdMob

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (sponsored posts inside the video
feed). To serve ads, Google collects:

- Your Android advertising ID
- IP address (used for approximate location only — we never collect
  your precise location)
- Device information and app-interaction data
- Diagnostic data

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent and region settings.
You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in
Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's own privacy policy applies
to this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 1.5 What we do NOT collect

- No precise location.
- No contacts, call logs, SMS, or files outside what you choose to upload.
- No health, financial, or biometric data.
- No separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK is built into the App.

## 2. How we use your information

- To run the App: accounts, profiles, feeds, likes, comments, follows,
  search, and notifications.
- To show your profile and videos to other users (public by design).
- To keep the App safe: reviewing reports and removing content that
  breaks the rules.
- To show ads, through Google AdMob (see section 1.4).

We do **not** sell your personal data to anyone.

## 3. Who we share data with

Only the service providers needed to run the App:

| Provider | What they do | Where | What they receive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database and file storage | India (ap-south-1) | Account data, videos, comments, photos |
| Render | Hosting for the App's backend server | Region not stated | Account data and content the backend handles |
| Google (AdMob) | Advertising, as described in section 1.4 | Global | Advertising ID, IP address, device data |

We do not share your data with any other third party. These providers process data only to provide their services to us.

Videos you post are public: anyone using the App can watch them, and share links to a video work outside the App too.

## 4. Your rights — what you can ask for

You can exercise these rights in the App where a path exists, or by emailing slayscroll73@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We acknowledge every request within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days.

- **Access — get a copy of your data.** In the App, go to Settings → Download my data and save the file it offers you. It contains your profile, videos, comments, likes, follows, blocks, and reports you filed. Or email us and ask for a copy.
- **Correction — fix something wrong.** Change your display name, bio, photo, and interests in the App (Profile → Edit profile). For anything else, email us.
- **Erasure — delete your account and data.** In the App, go to Settings → Delete account and confirm; or use the web deletion page at https://streamly-backend-aoin.onrender.com/delete-account. You can also email slayscroll73@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask for your account to be deleted. We will delete your account, profile, videos, comments, likes, and follows. Videos you posted are public, so copies may remain in caches or on other people's devices for a short time after deletion.
- **Delete your content** — you can delete your own videos and comments.
- **Block and report** — you can block any user and report any video or comment from inside the App.
- **Grievance** — if you have a complaint about your data or about content on the App, write to our Grievance Officer — Dheeraj Kumar Yadav, slayscroll73@gmail.com (see section 9).
- **Withdrawal of consent** — the App does not use consent-based processing beyond running your account: you must be 18 or over, and the App needs your account details to work at all. So withdrawing consent means deleting your account — use Settings → Delete account, or email us, and we will delete it.

**Data-protection contact (authorised person):** Dheeraj Kumar Yadav, slayscroll73@gmail.com — the same person as the Grievance Officer. Write to this address for any privacy request above.

**If your data is breached:** we will tell affected users without delay, and we will inform the Data Protection Board of India as the Rules require.

## 5. Data security and retention

- Passwords are stored only as hashes. All traffic between the App and its servers uses HTTPS.
- **Account data** — kept while your account exists, and deleted when your account is deleted (see section 4).
- **Videos and comments** — deleted when you delete them. Public copies may persist briefly in caches.
- **Reports** — kept for 90 days after a report is resolved, then deleted.
- **Deletion-request records** — kept as the admin audit trail of account deletions.
- **Server logs** — kept only as long as needed to run the service.
- There is no automatic retention purger yet — apart from account deletion, which removes your data when you ask for it, retention beyond the periods above is enforced manually for now.

## 6. Age — 18 and over

Slay & Scroll is for people aged **18 and over**. We do not knowingly
collect information from children under 18. Your date of birth is
collected at sign-up only to enforce the 18+ age check. If you believe
a person under 18 has created an account, email us at
slayscroll73@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

## 7. Permissions the App asks for

- **Internet** — to load videos and talk to the App's servers.
- **Photos and videos** — only the photos and videos you choose to
  upload (profile photo, video posts), picked through the system file
  picker.

## 9. Content complaints, copyright and grievance officer

- **Report content that breaks the rules** — report any video or comment from inside the App. A video or comment reported by three different users is hidden automatically until it is reviewed by a person.
- **Copyright** — if your video or other work was posted on Slay & Scroll without your permission, email the Grievance Officer below with a link to the video and proof that the work is yours. We will remove it promptly once we have checked the complaint.
- **Grievance Officer** — Dheeraj Kumar Yadav, **slayscroll73@gmail.com**. Complaints about content, copyright, or privacy are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 days.
- **Child safety** — Sexual content involving children is strictly prohibited on Slay & Scroll. We remove such content immediately when we learn of it and report it to the authorities through India's National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in). Our child-safety point of contact is our Grievance Officer — Dheeraj Kumar Yadav, slayscroll73@gmail.com.
- **Non-consensual intimate images** — Complaints about non-consensual intimate images, nudity, or morphed images of you are removed within 24 hours of your complaint.
- Content involving the sexual exploitation of children is removed immediately and reported to the authorities.
