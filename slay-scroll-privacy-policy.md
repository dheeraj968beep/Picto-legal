# Privacy Policy — Slay & Scroll

**Effective date: 25 November 2026**

Slay & Scroll ("the App") is a short-video app: you can watch short videos,
post your own, like and comment, follow creators, and discover new videos.
The App is operated by an individual developer based in India.

Contact for privacy questions and requests:

**Email: slayscroll73@gmail.com**

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Account information (you give this to us when you sign up)

- Email address
- Username
- Password — stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text
- Display name
- Date of birth — used only to check that you are 18 or older (see
  section 6). Your date of birth is never shown publicly and is never
  returned by the App's programming interface.
- Country — when you sign up, the App sends your device's region (for
  example, IN for India). We store it as your account country and show it
  on your profile's About sheet as "Account based in …". There is no
  option to change it in the App.
- Profile photo (optional)
- Bio (optional, up to 150 characters)
- Interests you pick during onboarding (optional)

### 1.2 Content you create

- Videos and photos you upload, and their captions. They are stored in
  cloud storage and shown to other users according to your account
  privacy and the audience you choose for the post.
- Likes, comments and replies, follows, and shares. Comments can include
  a photo or GIF you attach.
- Messages you send, including direct messages with photos, videos,
  voice notes, stickers, and replies to chains.
- Chain items you add, including photos, videos, captions, and link
  stickers.
- Battle chat messages, including photos and GIFs you send there.
- View counts on your videos.

### 1.3 Reports and blocks (safety)

- Reports you file about content or an account (the reason you pick and
  any note you add) and users you block. We use these to review content
  and keep the App safe. A video, comment, or chain reported by three
  different users is hidden automatically until it is reviewed.

### 1.4 Advertising — Google AdMob

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (sponsored posts inside the video
feed). To serve ads, Google collects:

- Your Android advertising ID
- IP address (used for approximate location only — we never collect
  your precise location)
- Device information and app-interaction data
- Diagnostic data

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent and region settings.
You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in
Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's own privacy policy applies
to this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 1.5 Monetisation and payments

- Creator subscriptions and gifts are paid through Google Play. Google
  processes the payment and confirms the purchase to us; the App never
  sees your card details.
- We store the purchase record needed to unlock the subscription or
  credit the gift (for example, the product, amount tier, status, and
  time).
- Creators who set up payouts submit payout details (bank transfer or
  PayPal). Payout details are stored encrypted, shown back only in
  masked form, and used only to pay that creator. We also store earnings
  and payout records for creators.

### 1.6 Support

- Questions you ask the help desk and support tickets you send are stored
  so the operator can read them and reply. A ticket can include your
  account email, username, subject, and message.

### 1.7 GIF search, notifications, and translation

- When you search for a GIF in comments or battle chat, your GIF search
  terms are sent to Giphy to fetch GIF results. A GIF you choose is
  downloaded and uploaded to our storage so it can be shown in the App.
- If notifications are on, Firebase gives the App a device push token. We
  store that token to send push notifications, such as likes, comments,
  follows, and messages. You can turn notifications off in the App or in
  your device settings.
- Caption and comment translation runs on your device. The App does not
  send the text to a translation server to translate it.

### 1.8 What we do NOT collect

- No precise location. Your account country comes from your device's
  region setting, not from GPS.
- No contacts, call logs, SMS, or files outside what you choose to upload.
- No health or biometric data.
- No card details — Google Play processes payments (see section 1.5).
- No separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK is built into the App.

## 2. How we use your information

- To run the App: accounts, profiles, feeds, likes, comments, follows,
  search, messages, chains, battles, and notifications.
- To show your profile and content to the audience allowed by your
  settings.
- To keep the App safe: reviewing reports and removing content that
  breaks the rules.
- To run creator monetisation: confirming Google Play purchases,
  crediting gifts and subscriptions, and paying creators who set up
  payouts.
- To answer support questions and tickets.
- To show ads, through Google AdMob (see section 1.4).

We do **not** sell your personal data to anyone.

## 3. Who we share data with

Only the service providers needed to run the App:

| Provider | What they do | Where | What they receive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database and file storage | India (ap-south-1) | Account data, videos, comments, photos, messages, and other content the App stores |

process data only to provide their services to us.

Content you make public can be watched by anyone using the App, and share
links to public content work outside the App too. Content limited by
privacy or subscriber settings is shown only to people allowed by those
settings.

## 4. Your rights — what you can ask for

You can exercise these rights in the App where a path exists, or by
emailing slayscroll73@gmail.com from the email address on your
account. We acknowledge every request within 24 hours and resolve it
within 15 days.

- **Access — get a copy of your data.** Email us and ask for a copy. It
  can include your profile, videos, comments, likes, follows, blocks, and
  reports you filed.
- **Correction — fix something wrong.** Change your display name, bio,
  photo, and interests in the App (Profile → Edit profile). Your account
  country is set at sign-up and cannot be changed in the App. For
  anything else, email us.
- **Erasure — delete your account and data.** In the App, go to
  Settings → Delete account and confirm; or use the web deletion page
  at https://streamly-backend-aoin.onrender.com/delete-account. You
  can also email slayscroll73@gmail.com from the email address on
  your account and ask for your account to be deleted. We will delete
  your account, profile, videos, comments, likes, and follows. Public
  copies may remain in caches or on other people's devices for a short
  time after deletion.
- **Delete your content** — you can delete your own videos and comments.
- **Block and report** — you can block any user and report content or an
  account from inside the App.
- **Grievance** — if you have a complaint about your data or about
  content on the App, write to our Grievance Officer — Dheeraj Kumar
  Yadav, slayscroll73@gmail.com (see section 9).
- **Withdrawal of consent** — the App does not use consent-based
  processing beyond running your account: you must be 18 or over, and
  the App needs your account details to work at all. So withdrawing
  consent means deleting your account — use Settings → Delete
  account, or email us, and we will delete it.

**Data-protection contact (authorised person):** Dheeraj Kumar Yadav,
slayscroll73@gmail.com — the same person as the Grievance Officer.
Write to this address for any privacy request above.

**If your data is breached:** we will tell affected users without
delay, and we will inform the Data Protection Board of India as the
Rules require.

## 5. Data security and retention

- Passwords are stored only as hashes. All traffic between the App and
  its servers uses HTTPS.
- **Account data** — kept while your account exists, and deleted when
  your account is deleted (see section 4).
- **Videos, photos, and comments** — deleted when you delete them. Public
  copies may persist briefly in caches.
- **Messages, chains, and battle chat** — kept while the conversation,
  chain, battle, or account exists, or until you delete the content
  where the App allows it. Live chains expire after 24 hours; saved
  highlights stay until removed or the account is deleted.
- **Support questions and tickets** — kept so we can reply and keep a
  record of the request.
- **Payout details and payout records** — kept while the creator keeps
  them or the account exists, and deleted with the account.
- **Push tokens** — kept while they are needed to send notifications to
  that device.
- **Reports** — kept for 90 days after a report is resolved, then
  deleted.
- **Deletion-request records** — kept as the admin audit trail of
  account deletions.
- **Server logs** — kept only as long as needed to run the service.
- There is no automatic retention purger yet — apart from account
  deletion, which removes your data when you ask for it, retention
  beyond the periods above is enforced manually for now.

## 6. Age — 18 and over

Slay & Scroll is for people aged **18 and over**. We do not knowingly
collect information from children under 18. Your date of birth is
collected at sign-up only to enforce the 18+ age check. If you believe
a person under 18 has created an account, email us at
slayscroll73@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

- **Notifications** — only if you allow them, to alert you about activity
