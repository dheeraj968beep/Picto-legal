# Privacy Policy — Slay & Scroll

**Effective date: 10 November 2026**

Slay & Scroll ("the App") is a short-video app: you can watch short videos,
post your own, like and comment, follow creators, and discover new videos.
The App is operated by an individual developer based in India.

Contact for privacy questions and requests:

**Email: dheeraj.8032007@gmail.com**

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Account information (you give this to us when you sign up)

- Email address
- Username
- Password — stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text
- Display name
- Date of birth — used only to check that you are 13 or older (see
  section 6). Your date of birth is never shown publicly and is never
  returned by the App's programming interface.
- Profile photo (optional)
- Bio (optional, up to 150 characters)
- Interests you pick during onboarding (optional)

### 1.2 Content you create

- Videos you upload, and their captions. Videos are stored in cloud
  storage and are public — anyone using the App can watch them.
- Likes, comments and replies, follows, and shares.
- View counts on your videos.

### 1.3 Reports and blocks (safety)

- Reports you file about a video or a comment (the reason you pick and
  any note you add) and users you block. We use these to review content
  and keep the App safe. A video or comment reported by three different
  users is hidden automatically until it is reviewed.

### 1.4 Advertising — Google AdMob

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (sponsored posts inside the video
feed). To serve ads, Google collects:

- Your Android advertising ID
- IP address (used for approximate location only — we never collect
  your precise location)
- Device information and app-interaction data
- Diagnostic data

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent and region settings.
You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in
Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's own privacy policy applies
to this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 1.5 What we do NOT collect

- No precise location.
- No contacts, call logs, SMS, or files outside what you choose to upload.
- No health, financial, or biometric data.
- No separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK is built into the App.

## 2. How we use your information

- To run the App: accounts, profiles, feeds, likes, comments, follows,
  search, and notifications.
- To show your profile and videos to other users (public by design).
- To keep the App safe: reviewing reports and removing content that
  breaks the rules.
- To show ads, through Google AdMob (see section 1.4).

We do **not** sell your personal data to anyone.

## 3. Who we share data with

Only the service providers needed to run the App:

- **Supabase** — database and file storage for accounts, videos,
  comments, and photos. Data is hosted in India (ap-south-1).
- **Render** — hosting for the App's backend server.
- **Google (AdMob)** — advertising, as described in section 1.4.

We do not share your data with any other third party. These providers
process data only to provide their services to us.

## 4. Your choices and rights

- **Edit your profile** — display name, bio, photo, and interests can
  be changed in the App (Profile → Edit profile).
- **Delete your content** — you can delete your own videos and comments.
- **Block and report** — you can block any user and report any video or
  comment from inside the App.
- **Delete your account** — email dheeraj.8032007@gmail.com from the
  email address on your account and ask for your account to be deleted.
  We will delete your account, profile, videos, comments, likes, and
  follows. Videos you posted are public, so copies may remain in
  caches or on other people's devices for a short time after deletion.

## 5. Data security and retention

- Passwords are stored only as hashes. All traffic between the App and
  its servers uses HTTPS.
- We keep your account data while your account exists, and delete it
  when you ask us to delete your account (see section 4).
- Videos and comments you delete are removed from our servers; public
  copies may persist briefly in caches.

## 6. Children

Slay & Scroll is for people aged **13 and over**. We do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13. Your date of birth is
collected at sign-up only to enforce the 13+ age check. If you believe
a child under 13 has created an account, email us at
dheeraj.8032007@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

## 7. Permissions the App asks for

- **Internet** — to load videos and talk to the App's servers.
- **Photos and videos** — only the photos and videos you choose to
  upload (profile photo, video posts), picked through the system file
  picker.

the App after a change means you accept the new policy.
